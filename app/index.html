    Missable trophies

    PowerPyx and GTABase agree: there is only one possibly missable trophy in GTA VI. Everything else can be finished after the story in free-roam. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so keep manual saves at the start of each chapter — the game offers 15 manual save slots.

    Save habits that protect the platinum

    1. Chapter 1, morning after Down to Earth: talk to Lucia in the kitchen at Jason’s house, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept on her meditation. It is gone after Chapter 1 and it is unconfirmed whether the trophy needs it — do it anyway.
    2. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Rotate the remaining slots for free-roam cleanup.
    3. Do time-sensitive stranger quests when they appear. A few quests carry an hourglass icon and fail after a while; PowerPyx says they are not needed for trophies, but Yago’s Race Against Yago feeds the Strangers & Freaks strand, so don’t leave it.
    4. Keep cheats off for the whole run. Cheats disable trophies and saving, and they only unlock after the story anyway.
    5. Turn on progress notifications (Settings > UI > HUD Notifications) so collectible and criminal-profile progress is always visible.
